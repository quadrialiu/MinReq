Call of Duty: Warzone

Infinity Ward / Raven Software

2020

Call of Duty: Warzone is a free-to-play battle royale game set in the Call of Duty universe. Players compete in massive multiplayer matches to be the last squad standing. The game features a large map with urban and rural areas, vehicles, and dynamic events.

Warzone offers a variety of gameplay modes including standard battle royale, Plunder, and limited-time events. Players can use a wide range of weapons, loadouts, and tactical strategies to survive and dominate opponents.

The game is continually updated with new content, weapons, and seasonal events, keeping the gameplay fresh for both casual and competitive players.
