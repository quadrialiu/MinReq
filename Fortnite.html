Fortnite

Epic Games

2017

Fortnite is a massively popular online multiplayer game featuring a Battle Royale mode, where 100 players fight to be the last one standing on an ever-shrinking map. The game is known for its vibrant graphics, building mechanics, and constant content updates.

Players can build structures, scavenge weapons, and compete in solo or team-based matches. Fortnite also includes creative modes for building custom maps and mini-games, as well as collaborative events featuring popular franchises and live concerts.

The game is free-to-play and regularly updated with new seasons, challenges, skins, and gameplay features, keeping the community engaged and evolving.
