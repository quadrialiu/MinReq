Baldur's Gate 3

Larian Studios

2023

Baldur's Gate 3 is a turn-based RPG set in the Dungeons & Dragons universe. Players create a character and navigate the world of Faerûn, making choices that impact the story, relationships, and combat outcomes.

The game features rich character customization, strategic turn-based combat, a party system, and branching storylines that respond to player decisions. Exploration, dialogue, and tactical encounters are at the core of the experience.

With a deep narrative, expansive world, and multiplayer cooperative mode, Baldur's Gate 3 brings classic D&D adventures to life with modern mechanics and stunning visuals.
