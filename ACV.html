Assassin’s Creed Valhalla

Ubisoft

2020

Assassin’s Creed Valhalla is an open-world action role-playing game set during the Viking Age. Players take on the role of Eivor, a legendary Viking raider seeking glory and a new home in the lands of England.

The game features large-scale raids, brutal combat, deep character progression, and a massive open world spanning Norway, England, and mythical realms.

Valhalla blends stealth, exploration, and RPG mechanics while continuing the Assassin’s Creed storyline with choices that shape Eivor’s journey.
