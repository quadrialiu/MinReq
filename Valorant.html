Valorant

Riot Games

2020

Valorant is a tactical first-person shooter combining precise gunplay with unique character abilities. Players compete in 5v5 matches where strategy, coordination, and skill are key to winning rounds.

The game features a roster of "agents," each with distinct abilities that influence the flow of matches. Teamwork and communication are essential for success in competitive modes, while casual modes allow experimentation and fun.

Released in 2020 by Riot Games, Valorant has quickly become a popular esports title, offering frequent updates, new agents, and map rotations.
