League of Legends

Riot Games

2009

League of Legends is a competitive multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game where two teams of five champions face off to destroy the enemy Nexus. Each player selects a unique champion with distinct abilities and roles.

Matches focus on strategy, teamwork, map control, and mechanical skill. Players earn gold and experience to upgrade abilities and purchase items during fast-paced matches.

League of Legends is free-to-play and constantly updated with new champions, balance changes, seasonal events, and esports competitions worldwide.
