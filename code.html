About the Game

Grand Theft Auto V is an open-world action-adventure game set in the fictional state of San Andreas, centered around Los Santos. Players experience the intertwined stories of three protagonists while exploring a massive world filled with missions, heists, and side activities.

GTA V also includes GTA Online, offering cooperative and competitive multiplayer gameplay with players worldwide.

💡 Can I run it? If your PC has 8GB RAM and a GTX 660 or better, GTA V will run smoothly on medium settings.
