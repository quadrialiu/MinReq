Forza Horizon 5

Playground Games

2021

Forza Horizon 5 is an open-world racing game set in a vibrant and diverse recreation of Mexico, featuring deserts, jungles, beaches, cities, and active volcanoes.

Players can drive hundreds of licensed cars, compete in races and events, complete challenges, and explore the world solo or with friends online.

With dynamic seasons, weather effects, and frequent content updates, Forza Horizon 5 delivers a fast-paced and visually stunning racing experience.
