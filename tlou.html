The Last of Us Part I

Naughty Dog

2022

The Last of Us Part I is a narrative-driven action-adventure game set in a post-apocalyptic world devastated by a fungal infection. Players follow Joel, a hardened survivor, as he escorts a young girl named Ellie across a dangerous United States.

The game focuses on emotional storytelling, survival mechanics, and intense combat encounters against hostile humans and infected creatures.

This remake enhances visuals, animations, and gameplay systems while preserving the original story that defined the franchise.
