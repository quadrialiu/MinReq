Helldivers 2

Arrowhead Game Studios

2024

Helldivers 2 is a cooperative third-person shooter where players enlist as elite soldiers fighting to protect Super Earth from hostile alien threats across the galaxy.

The game emphasizes teamwork, friendly fire, tactical combat, and chaotic large-scale battles against overwhelming enemies using powerful weapons and stratagems.

With online co-op for up to four players, Helldivers 2 delivers intense action, emergent gameplay moments, and satirical sci-fi storytelling.
