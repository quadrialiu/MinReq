Elden Ring

FromSoftware

2022

Elden Ring is an open-world action RPG developed by FromSoftware in collaboration with George R.R. Martin. Players explore the vast Lands Between, battling formidable enemies, uncovering secrets, and customizing their character with weapons, magic, and abilities.

The game features a rich narrative, challenging combat, and intricate world-building. Players can traverse diverse environments, encounter massive bosses, and engage in strategic gameplay that rewards skill and exploration.

Elden Ring offers deep freedom and replayability, making every adventure unique, with multiple endings, optional bosses, and cooperative multiplayer features.
