God of War: Ragnarök

Santa Monica Studio

2022

God of War: Ragnarök continues the journey of Kratos and his son Atreus in the world of Norse mythology. Players explore the Nine Realms, battling gods, monsters, and giants as they uncover the prophecy of Ragnarök.

The game features cinematic storytelling, strategic combat with both axe and shield, puzzle-solving, and deep character development. It expands upon the critically acclaimed mechanics of the 2018 God of War reboot.

Players will experience epic boss battles, dynamic environments, and a gripping narrative about family, destiny, and the end of the Norse gods.
